# Nav List

`<craft-nav-list>` wraps a set of [Nav Item](?path=/docs/components-nav-item--docs)s in a real list.

<Canvas of={NavListStories.Default} sourceState="shown" />

It contributes two things and nothing else: the `<ul>` semantics, and the spacing between items. The items carry their own appearance.

## Why the List Matters

A run of links in a `<div>` is announced one at a time, with no sense of how many there are or where you are among them. Inside a list, a screen reader says "list, 5 items" and numbers them as you move.

That is the whole reason this component exists rather than a wrapper `<div>` — and it is why nav items belong inside one even when the visual result looks identical.
