# Field Group

`<craft-field-group>` lays [Field](?path=/docs/form-controls-field--docs)s out on a twelve-column grid.

<Canvas of={FieldGroupStories.Default} sourceState="shown" />

Children take a full row by default, which is the right default for a form: full-width fields are the easiest to scan down.

## Widths

A `width-*` class on a child gives it a fraction of the row:

<Canvas of={FieldGroupStories.Widths} sourceState="shown" />

The available fractions are `width-25`, `width-33`, `width-50`, `width-66`, and `width-75` — quarters, thirds, and halves of the twelve columns.

## Container Queries, Not Media Queries

The breakpoints respond to the width of the group itself, not the viewport.

That means a group inside a narrow pane stacks even on a wide screen:

<Canvas of={FieldGroupStories.NarrowContainer} sourceState="shown" />

This is what you want for a component that has no idea where it will be placed — a form in a slideout, a modal, or a narrow sidebar gets a sensible layout without anyone configuring it.

Between roughly 25rem and 50rem, `width-25` widens to a half rather than collapsing straight to full, so a row of four short fields becomes two rows of two.
